This game is called Bouncy arena.

It's an exciting mix of a gladiator and monster fighter game.
In the main menu, you have three options. 
The first is "Start" which forwards you to the stats customization screen, where you have 5 points available at the start and five categories that you can assign any number of points 
to: Strength, Agility, Defence, Attack and Stamina. After each death, you get one more skills point to use. Once you select your stats, you will be put to the arena against the boss. The boss has a melee and ranged attack
and varying health, fire rate and idle rate, that you can change in settings. The boss will follow you around the map until you kill him. Once you do, you have won. You also get points for every 30s surviving.
The second option is "Settings" which lets you customize the difficulty of the game by changing these variables: Boss HP, Boss Projectiles number, Boss fire rate, Boss idle rate (how often the boss goes idle and stops 
attacking for a while, the amount of skills points at the start and your base Player HP. 
The third button in the main menu is "Back" which closes the game.

It's a simple but fun game.
Have fun!


How do you play it?

Select the stats, for example better strength, agility, defence and then fight the boss in the  arena!


Controls 

AD/arrows for movement

Left mouse button to attack

Space to jump

Shift to sprint
