An open-source 2D action adventure game made in **Godot Engine** during the **Hack Club Haven** event.

## About the Game
Built around the theme **"Bounce Back"**, *Project_Haven* throws you back in time from the present day into a medieval world. Playing as a knight, you must navigate through unfamiliar lands, overcome deadly obstacles, interact with various NPCs, and battle bosses to find your way back home.

### Key Features
* **Time-Travel Premise:** Step out of the modern era into the past as a knight.
* **Exploration & NPCs:** Meet different characters along your journey who shape your path.
* **Obstacles & Boss Fights:** Dodge traps, conquer platforming challenges, and defeat tough enemies.

## Controls
* **Move:** `W A S D` / `Arrow Keys`
* **Attack:** `Left Mouse Button`

## Tech Stack
* **Engine:** Godot 4
* **Language:** GDScript
